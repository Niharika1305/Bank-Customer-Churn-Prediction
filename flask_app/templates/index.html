ABOUT CHURNSENSE AI

Intelligent Customer Churn Analysis

ChurnSense AI is a machine learning based customer churn prediction platform designed to help identify customers who may be at risk of leaving a bank.

By analyzing important customer information such as credit score, age, tenure, account balance, number of products, estimated salary, geography, credit card ownership, and membership activity, the system estimates the likelihood of customer churn.

01

How ChurnSense AI Works

The application uses multiple machine learning classification models to analyze customer data and generate churn predictions. Users can select an individual model or compare predictions from all available models.

When multiple models are selected, ChurnSense AI provides a model-wise comparison along with estimated churn probabilities, making it easier to understand how different models evaluate the same customer.

02

Why Customer Churn Matters

Customer retention is an important factor for banks and financial institutions. Identifying customers who may be likely to leave can help organizations understand potential risks and take appropriate retention measures.

ChurnSense AI provides a simple and interactive way to explore these predictions through a user-friendly web interface.
